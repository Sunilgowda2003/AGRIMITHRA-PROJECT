🌊 Famous Rivers of Karnataka 🏞️

Karnataka Rivers – Life-giving Water Sources!

The rivers flowing through the state play an important role in agriculture, drinking water, culture, and history.

Kaveri River

The Kaveri River, which originates in Talakaveri in Kodagu district and flows through many districts of Karnataka, is important for agriculture..

Tungabhadra River

The Tungabhadra River, formed by the confluence is a witness to the ancient history of Hampi and is supportive of agriculture.

Krishna River

The Krishna River, originating from Maharashtra and flowing through North Karnataka, plays a key role in farmers' agriculture.
