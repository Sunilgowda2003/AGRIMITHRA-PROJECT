Relevant information about Indian and Karnataka scientists who have made significant contributions in the field of agriculture

Dr. M. S. Swaminathan

The Father of the Green Revolution in India. Contributed to increasing agricultural production through high-quality seeds, chemical fertilizers, and irrigation.

Dr. Varghese Kurian.

"The Father of the 'White Revolution.' Made India self-sufficient in milk production through Operation Flood.

Dr. R. H. Richharia.

Expert in Indian soil conservation. Has done significant work to preserve local varieties.

Dr. Subrahmanyam Nagarajan.

A key scientist of the Wheat Revolution in India. Played a major role in developing rust-resistant wheat varieties.

Dr. M. Mahadevappa.

Contributed to the cultivation of high-yielding rice varieties. Former Vice-Chancellor of Agricultural University, Dharwad.

Dr. M. H. Marigowda.

The person who scientifically developed horticulture in Karnataka. Considered the 'Father of Horticulture' for his contributions to the field.
