🌾 Path of Agricultural Development in India 🚜

⏳ Agricultural Revolutions in India and Their Impact! 🌍

🌿 Transformation of Livelihood: From Hunting to Agriculture (25000 BCE - 15000 BCE)

Early humans hunted for food and gathered forest herbs. Over time, due to changes in climate and environment, they began to observe naturally growing plants and gradually learned the techniques of sowing and crop cultivation.

Tools Used: Stone arrows, wooden plows, manual sowing, and canal irrigation .

🌱Early Agvriculture (9000 BCE - 2500 BCE)

Early agriculture in India began along the banks of the Indus River. In the Harappan civilization, crops like wheat, barley, cotton, and sesame were cultivated.

Tools Used Manual plowing, canal irrigation, and farming with the help of oxen. .

🏺 Ancient Agriculture(1500 BCE - 500 CE)

In Vedic period agriculture, monsoon rain-based farming was predominant. Farmers cultivated wheat, rice, and cotton. .

Tools Used: Rope canal irrigation, rope-drawn bullock carts, and handheld iron tools.

🏰 Medieval Agriculture (1200 CE - 1700 CE)

During the Sultanate and Mughal empires, the land revenue system was developed. New fruit and flower crops were introduced.

Tools Used: Tank irrigation, fieldwork with the help of oxen, and tools made of bronze and iron.

🇮🇳 Colonial Agriculture (1757 CE - 1947 CE)

During British ruleCash crops (Coffee, coconut, cotton) cultivation was encouraged to increase. Increased taxes on farmers became a problem.

Tools Used: Horse carts, iron tools, and the very first railway transportation for market connectivity

🌾 Green Revolution (1960 CE - 1990 CE)

MS Swaminathan Under his guidance, the use of high-yield seeds, chemical fertilizers, and irrigation increased significantly in India.

Tools Used: Tractors, tube well irrigation, hybrid seeds, and enriched fertilizers.

🤖 Modern Agriculture (2000 CE - Present)

Smart technologies have entered agriculture. Drone technology, AI (Artificial Intelligence), IoT, and sustainable farming practices are growing.

Tools Used:Drones, AI-based weather forecasting technology, IoT-based irrigation, and smart tractors.
